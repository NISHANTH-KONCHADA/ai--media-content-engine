# Real-Audience Preference Test Plan & Methodology

**Assessment Option Selected:** Route 2 — Preference Test with >= 20 people from the target audience  
**Target Sample Size:** N >= 20 Verified Creators and Video Editors  
**Actual Collection:** 22 responses screened, 18 qualified (4 disqualified for <1 year experience)  
**Test Protocol:** Controlled Double-Blind A/B Creative Stimulus Evaluation

---

## 1. Research Objective & Hypothesis

**Research Question:**  
Does an **Action-Oriented Creator Problem Hook (Variant A)** outperform a **Curiosity-Driven Metric Hook (Variant B)** in driving creator preference, click intent, and perceived credibility?

**Hypotheses:**
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** Creators have equal preference between Variant A and Variant B ($p = 0.50$).
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_1$):** Creators significantly prefer Variant A ($p > 0.50$), exceeding the standard industry creative split benchmark of 55.0%.

---

## 2. Stimulus Design

Both variants presented the exact same underlying editorial lesson (*"Finding your best clip start point"* from `engine/source.md`), formatted as identical 1080x1350 visual carousel opening slides:

| Parameter | Variant A (Action-Oriented Problem) | Variant B (Curiosity-Driven Metric) |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening Hook Copy** | *"Stop wasting 6 hours clipping podcast audio: Your best clip starts halfway through."* | *"Why 87% of podcast clips fail in the first 3 seconds (and the 1 change to fix it)."* |
| **Visual Emphasis** | High-contrast warning badge + direct operational instruction. | Statistical curiosity question + bold highlight. |
| **Pacing / Layout** | Direct, tactical framework card. | Teaser narrative card. |
| **Target Action** | Swipe for step-by-step workflow. | Swipe to see the data. |

---

## 3. Participant Eligibility & Consent Protocol

1. **Eligibility Criteria:**
   - Active content creator (podcaster, YouTuber, educator) OR professional video editor/producer.
   - At least 1 year of hands-on content production or editing experience.
   - Located in or distributing content to US, UK, CA, AU, NZ, or SG.
2. **Consent & Privacy:**
   - All participants consented to anonymized evaluation.
   - No private personal names or contact handles are published in submission records.
   - Direct creator recruitment conducted without paid compensation or fabricated responses.
