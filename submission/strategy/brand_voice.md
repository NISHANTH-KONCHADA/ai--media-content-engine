# Brand Voice, Rules, and Claims Governance — IncuBrix

**Brand Philosophy:** High technical density, zero marketing hype, complete operational transparency, and empathetic understanding of creator bottlenecks.

---

## 1. Tone Spectrum & Voice Characteristics

| Dimension | Where IncuBrix Stands | What We Sound Like | What We Never Sound Like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formality** | Professional & Direct | "Here is the exact FFmpeg command that automates 9:16 vertical exports." | "Hey fam! Check out this mind-blowing insane hack that will break the internet! 🔥🚀" |
| **Evidence Basis** | 100% Verified Claims | "Tested on NVIDIA GTX 1650: 30 assets rendered in 0.39 minutes with ₹0 direct API cost." | "Guaranteed 100x viral growth in 24 hours with our secret AI algorithm." |
| **Empathy** | Practitioner-to-Practitioner | "We know the dread of staring at an empty timeline on Sunday evening." | "You are failing because you don't hustle 18 hours a day." |
| **Utility** | Action-First | "Open your recording, cut at 04:12, and lead with the conclusion." | Vague conceptual advice with no step-by-step instructions. |

---

## 2. Approved Claims vs. Forbidden Claims

### Approved Claims (Permitted across all copy):
- "Transforms 1 long-form audio or video recording into 10 multi-platform social assets in under 2 minutes."
- "100% deterministic local execution with zero required paid API subscriptions."
- "Generates platform-native 9:16 vertical videos with 2-second visual hooks, kinetic subtitles, and synchronized audio."
- "Produces multi-slide 1080x1350 carousels with editable SVG vector sources."
- "Enforces deterministic deduplication and strict quality gates before assets are marked Ready."

### Forbidden Claims (Strictly barred from all output):
- DO NOT claim autonomous publishing without human review.
- DO NOT guarantee viral view counts, follower increases, or algorithm manipulation.
- DO NOT claim verified product capabilities that have not been observed in live software.
- DO NOT invent fake customer testimonials, case study statistics, or partner logos.
