# Target Creator Personas — IncuBrix 2027

**Geographic Focus:** United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore  
**Language:** English (Native / Professional)

---

## Persona 1: The Solo Video Podcaster & YouTuber

- **Profile:** Independent creator hosting a weekly video podcast or long-form educational YouTube show (10k–100k audience).
- **Day-to-Day Reality:** Spends 4 hours recording and interviewing guests, followed by 12–15 hours editing, cutting video clips for Shorts/Reels, generating transcripts, writing captions, and posting manually across 3 platforms.
- **Core Frustration:** Extreme burnout. Wants to expand their multi-platform reach, but cannot afford a full-time video editor ($2,000–$4,000/mo) and is tired of paying multiple $40/mo cloud AI subscriptions with mediocre quality.
- **What Converts Them:** Showing them how 1 podcast recording automatically produces 10 ready-to-post short videos and carousels with accurate captions and hooks in under 2 minutes on their existing laptop.
- **Primary Channels:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels.

---

## Persona 2: The B2B Expert Educator & Founder

- **Profile:** Agency founder, technical consultant, or B2B SaaS leader using LinkedIn and webinars to generate high-ticket consulting or enterprise software leads.
- **Day-to-Day Reality:** Conducts monthly client webinars or deep-dive keynote presentations. Struggles to translate dense technical slides into punchy, consumable LinkedIn carousels.
- **Core Frustration:** Refuses generic AI "broetry" or superficial marketing fluff that damages their professional authority. Demands accurate diagrams, clean typography, and verified product claims.
- **What Converts Them:** Visual multi-slide framework carousels (1080x1350) that respect technical nuance, cite source evidence, and maintain strict brand colors.
- **Primary Channels:** LinkedIn, X.

---

## Persona 3: The Boutique Content Operations Lead & Video Editor

- **Profile:** Creative producer, junior editor, or content manager handling 3–5 client creator channels.
- **Day-to-Day Reality:** Juggling raw footage, Premiere Pro timelines, client revisions, broken tracking links, and disjointed Google Drive folders.
- **Core Frustration:** Client confidentiality constraints (strict NDAs preventing uploading raw footage to public third-party AI clouds), plus tool subscription fatigue eating into agency margins.
- **What Converts Them:** A local-first, zero-API-cost batch processing engine that exports ready-to-schedule CSVs and organized asset folders matching client brand DNA with 100% data privacy.
- **Primary Channels:** LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, TikTok.
