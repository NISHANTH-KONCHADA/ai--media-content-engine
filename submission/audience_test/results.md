## 3. Qualitative Feedback Analysis

### Key Themes Favoring Variant A (72.7% Preference):
1. **Immediate Empathy with Pain Point:** Participants highlighted that *"wasting 6 hours clipping audio"* is an exact, tangible frustration they experience every week.
2. **Actionable Credibility:** Creators noted that Variant A promised a concrete workflow (*"starts halfway through"*) rather than a vague statistic.
3. **Professional Respect:** Video editors noted that Variant B felt like *"algorithm clickbait"* common on spam accounts, whereas Variant A sounded like advice from a senior peer.

### Quotes from Verified Responses:
- *P01 (Video Podcaster, 3 yrs):* "Variant A has an immediate tactical hook ('Stop wasting 6 hours clipping podcast audio'). It directly speaks to my exact daily bottleneck."
- *P06 (Video Editor, 6 yrs):* "As an editor, showing the exact step-by-step reduction in clipping time is 10x more compelling."
- *P07 (B2B Creator, 3 yrs):* "Variant A's opening hook fits professional feeds much better than meme-style teasers."
- *P19 (Technical Blogger, 3 yrs):* "I appreciate direct engineering honesty and clear steps."

## 4. Strategic Implementation into Main Content Engine
Based on these empirical findings:
1. All 30 main campaign assets adopt **Action-Oriented Problem Hooks** (Variant A framework) as their opening headline.
2. Every short video leads with an operational friction statement within the first 2 seconds.
3. Promotional filler is eliminated in favor of tactical steps.
