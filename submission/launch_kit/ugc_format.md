# Creator-Collaboration Format: "The Repurposing Challenge"

**Status:** Ready to run (Week 2+ of launch plan)  
**Format Type:** User-generated content loop with creator co-ownership  
**Cost:** ₹0 (organic only; no paid amplification required)

---

## 1. Concept

Invite one micro-creator (1k–50k audience, video podcaster or editor) per week to take **the same 60-second source recording** and produce their own repurposed clip using the IncuBrix workflow template. IncuBrix publishes a stitched "two editors, one recording" carousel; the creator publishes their own version natively on their channel.

## 2. Why It Fits This Audience

- Creators respond to *process*, not product ads — watching a real peer clip the same source is proof, not promotion.
- Both parties get content: the creator gets a workflow asset + exposure; IncuBrix gets authentic third-party demonstration without scripted testimonials.
- Zero rights risk: the source recording is owned/licensed for the challenge, and each participant signs a one-line reuse agreement covering the stitched output.

## 3. Operating Mechanics (45 min/week, Growth Engineer)

| Step | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Invite (Mon) | DM 3 target creators with the challenge brief + source clip link. First "yes" wins the slot. | 1 confirmed creator |
| 2. Kit (Tue) | Send the challenge kit: 60s source recording, one-page workflow template, UTM'd submission link. | Creator kit |
| 3. Collect (Thu) | Creator returns their clip + 1 sentence on what they changed. | UGC clip + quote |
| 4. Publish (Fri) | Post the "A/B edit" carousel on LinkedIn/IG crediting the creator; creator posts their version same day. | 2 native posts |

## 4. Compliance Guardrails

- **Consent:** Written reuse permission covers the creator's clip, name, and handle; nothing is published without it.
- **No fake participation:** Only creators who actually return an edit are featured; skipped weeks are logged honestly in `metrics-tracking.csv`.
- **AI labelling:** Platform-required AI labels applied wherever the engine rendered frames.
- **No compensation claims:** Participation is unpaid and disclosed as such.

## 5. Measurement

- Primary: completed edits/week (target: 1), cross-posted reach (target: +25% vs solo posts), UTM clicks from creator posts.
- Kill criterion: if <1 confirmed creator for 2 consecutive weeks, replace with a recorded "community teardown" format.
