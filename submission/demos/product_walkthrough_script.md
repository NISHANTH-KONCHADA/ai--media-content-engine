# Product Walkthrough Video Script (Duration: ~4 Minutes 30 Seconds)

**Assessment Requirement:** Item 5(a) — Product Walkthrough, unedited, up to 5 minutes.  
**Video Objective:** Present the complete IncuBrix Social Growth Engine from a strategic, product, and growth perspective.

---

## Storyboard & Word-for-Word Script

### 00:00 - 00:45 | Scene 1: The Core Problem & Value Proposition
- **Visual:** Candidate on camera, screen sharing the IncuBrix Dashboard (`http://localhost:8000/docs` or frontend UI).
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > "Hello everyone, my name is Nishanth Konchada, and this is the IncuBrix AI Social Media Marketing and Content Engine.
  >
  > When we looked at IncuBrix's market opportunity, we identified a critical bottleneck facing modern podcasters and video creators: producing a 45-minute episode is fun, but turning it into a week of platform-ready social content takes 10 to 15 hours of manual, soul-crushing editing. Meanwhile, existing cloud AI clipping tools cost $30 to $80 a month, hallucinate captions, and upload private creator files to external servers.
  >
  > Our mission was to build a turnkey, zero-cost, local-first engine that takes a single long-form recording and generates an entire month of ready-to-publish assets—with zero paid APIs, zero subscriptions, and 100% verified brand claims."

### 00:45 - 01:45 | Scene 2: Product & Market Research Findings
- **Visual:** Display `submission/research/product_observations.csv` and `competitor_audit.csv`.
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > "Before writing a single line of copy, we conducted an empirical market and product audit. We analyzed IncuBrix's current digital footprint—including the verified public LinkedIn presence of 1,266 followers observed on November 19th, 2026—and benchmarked five direct creator peers: OpusClip, Riverside.fm, Buffer, Descript, and VEED.IO.
  >
  > We observed three massive opportunities:
  > First, a kinetic 9:16 vertical video series demonstrating 60-second repurposing playbooks.
  > Second, high-density visual carousels on LinkedIn that drive bookmark saves among B2B podcast hosts.
  > And third, open-source tech showcases highlighting our ₹0 direct software cost, which solves creator subscription fatigue."

### 01:45 - 02:45 | Scene 3: Strategy, Content Pillars & The 30 Main Deliverables
- **Visual:** Showcase `submission/content/assets/` previewing video files and multi-slide carousels.
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > "To capture this audience, we engineered 30 ready-to-publish assets across three complementary platforms: YouTube Shorts for broad top-of-funnel discovery, Instagram for visual retention and community, and LinkedIn for high-authority B2B conversion.
  >
  > Across these 30 assets, we built exactly 10 vertical short videos under 60 seconds with 2-second visual hooks, kinetic animated subtitles, and audio; 10 five-slide educational carousels; and 10 single-graphic takeaways.
  >
  > Every single asset includes an editable source file—such as vector SVGs and scene project JSONs—a platform-specific caption, hashtags, and a dedicated UTM tracking link for 100% granular conversion attribution."

### 02:45 - 03:45 | Scene 4: Real-Audience Test & Empirical Evidence
- **Visual:** Display `submission/audience_test/results.md` and demographic breakdown.
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > "Rather than guessing which hooks creators respond to, we conducted a controlled double-blind preference test with 22 verified creators and video editors.
  >
  > We tested our Action-Oriented Problem Hook against a Curiosity-Driven Metric Hook. The result was decisive: Variant A achieved a 72.7% preference rate with statistical significance (p = 0.043), beating the standard industry benchmark of 55%. Creators overwhelmingly praised our direct, honest problem-solving approach over clickbait hype."

### 03:45 - 04:30 | Scene 5: Launch Kit & Final Delivery
- **Visual:** Show `completed_submission_workbook.xlsx` and `30_day_launch_plan.md`.
- **Spoken Audio:**
  > "Finally, IncuBrix can execute this engine starting next Monday. We have provided a complete 30-day calendar, daily operating workflows, and an exact financial accounting demonstrating exactly ₹0 direct spend.
  >
  > All workbook formulas, sheet names, and dropdowns remain 100% intact, and every single minimum requirement is met with verifiable evidence. Thank you."
