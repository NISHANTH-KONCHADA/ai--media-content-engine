# Multi-Platform Strategy & Distribution Architecture

**Selected Primary Platforms:**  
1. **YouTube Shorts**  
2. **Instagram (Reels & Feed Carousels)**  
3. **LinkedIn (B2B Multi-Slide Documents & Posts)**

---

## 1. Why These Three Platforms?

| Platform | Strategic Rationale for Target Creator Audience | Native Format & Aspect Ratio | Content Pillars Deployed | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts** | Highest organic discovery algorithm for video podcasters. Viewers are already watching video content and actively looking for production tips and software tutorials. | 9:16 Vertical Video (1080x1920), duration 12–45s, bold hook in first 2 seconds, synchronized captions. | Zero-Cost Hacks, Workflow Systems, Creator Growth Science. | 3 posts/week (Mon, Wed, Fri) |
| **Instagram** | Primary hub for visual culture, community building, and direct messaging with solo creators and editing freelancers. | **LinkedIn** | Premier channel for expert-led educators, founders, and B2B podcast hosts in US, UK, SG, and AU. High decision-maker concentration and unparalleled bookmark/repost velocity on multi-slide document carousels. | 4:5 Vertical Carousels (1080x1350) + Long-Form Text Breakdowns + 1:1 Framework Graphics. | Engineering Proof, B2B Growth Science, Workflow Architecture. | 4 posts/week (Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu) |

## 2. Platform-Specific Publishing Rules

1. **Never Clone Captions Verbatim:**
   - **LinkedIn:** Starts with a single-line contrarian hook, followed by spaced bullet points, a direct tactical takeaway, and a conversational closing question to encourage comment discussion. Minimal hashtags (3–4 targeted tags).
   - **Instagram:** Focuses on mobile scroll-stopping imagery, step-by-step swipe instructions, and 5–8 targeted niche community hashtags (`#CreatorEconomy #VideoEditor #PodcastProduction`).
   - **YouTube Shorts:** Fast, high-energy text hook, explicit cue to read the pinned comment for the full template link, and 3–4 discovery tags.

2. **UTM Attribution Architecture:**
   Every link follows the standardized naming taxonomy:
   `https://www.incubrix.com/contact?utm_source={platform}&utm_medium={format}&utm_campaign=growth_engine_2027&utm_content={asset_id}`
   This enables 100% granular tracking of clicks and sign-ups back to the specific creative asset and platform format.
